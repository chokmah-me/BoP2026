# Background: The Origin of Balance of Power 2026

## From the Amiga 1000 to a New Era of Great Power Competition

**Balance of Power 2026** started with a game I played as a teen on my Amiga 1000.

It was the mid 1980s. The Cold War was still on. I had already traveled behind the Iron Curtain and seen what life looked like on the other side. That experience stayed with me. Then I found *Balance of Power* (1985) by Chris Crawford.

I spent many hours on that game. It let you play as the United States or the Soviet Union. You managed crises. You tried to avoid nuclear war. The tension felt real. Small choices could lead to big problems. I learned early that great power politics was not just about weapons. It was also about perception, timing, and risk.

Years later the world changed. The Soviet Union collapsed. Then new powers rose. China became strong. Other countries gained influence. The old bipolar world turned into something more complex. I started to think about that old Amiga game again. The basic questions had not gone away. They had just become harder.

## Why I Built BoP2026

I wanted to make a new version of that game for today.

The original game only had two sides. Today there are many powers that matter: the United States, China, Russia, India, the European Union, the Gulf states, and Iran. Competition now happens in many areas at once. Military power still matters. But so do economics, cyber tools, information, diplomacy, and what happens inside countries.

BoP2026 lets you explore these modern problems. You can run many simulations. You can test different ideas. You can see how one crisis can turn into another. It is meant for students and researchers who want to understand how the world works now.

## The Original Game

*Balance of Power* from 1985 is still worth playing. It was ahead of its time.

You can play it today using an Amiga emulator. Here is the easiest way:

1. Download Balance of Power from My Abandonware: https://www.myabandonware.com/game/balance-of-power-5e
2. Use the free emulator **FS-UAE** (available for Windows, Mac, and Linux): https://fs-uae.net/

FS-UAE is simple to set up and runs the original game well.
